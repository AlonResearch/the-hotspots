## 1. Project Invariants

- **Project**: The Hotspots (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026)
- **Challenge**: Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots
- **Core Problem**: Reconciling the split satellite records between MODIS (Terra/Aqua, 1km, 2000-present) and VIIRS (Suomi-NPP/NOAA-20/21, 375m/750m, 2012-present) to create a consistent, anomaly-aware burning activity calendar.
- **Team Composition** (3 members, per the team): Arthur Alonso (AI, UI/UX), Nurbanu Koc (science and pitch), Bekhzod Anvarov (programming and AI). Event: Turin local event.
- **Architecture Philosophy**: Low-friction, first-principles problem solving. Do not commit to unverified stacks, pre-made schemas, or premature technical solutions before explicit design decisions are agreed upon.

## 3e. Raw Data Ingestion (decided and verified, 2026-11-14)

- **Paths (two, no overlap)**: (1) FIRMS **Archive Download** (web form, Earthdata or email-code login), worldwide, CSV, one request per product and calendar year, covering MODIS 2000-11 to 2026-11-14, S-NPP 2012-01-20 on, NOAA-20 2018-04 on, NOAA-21 2023-03 on. This is the primary source. (2) FIRMS **Area API** script, worldwide monthly CSV, used only for months the archive lacks. Data is not in git (`data/` is git-ignored).
- **Archive facts (measured)**: world area is one option; sources are checkboxes, each becoming its own request row; at most one calendar year per world request; no NRT/Standard choice; the account cap counts requests not yet Processed (hit at 22 rows, accepted again once earlier ones left the queue). Processed requests list a public zip link (`/data/download/DL_FIRE_<source>_<id>.zip`, no login needed), so they download with curl. 55 requests (2000-11 to 2026-11-14, one per product and year, listed in `scripts/firms_archive_links.csv`) all reached Processed on 2026-11-14 within about 2.5 hours. A zip holds `fire_archive_*.csv` and, for 2026, `fire_nrt_*.csv`. Layout: zips in `data/raw/archive-zips/`, extracted to `data/raw/archive/DL_FIRE_<source>_<id>/`.
- **Differences between archive and API files (measured)**: `acq_time` zero-padded in the archive and an integer in the API files; S-NPP is `SNPP/SNPP` in the archive and `N/VIIRS` in the API; archive NRT files have no `type` column. See `pipeline/README.md`, step normalize.
- **Script**: `scripts/download_firms.py` (Python stdlib only, `python -I scripts/download_firms.py`). Reads `FIRMS_MAP_KEY` from the environment or `.env`. Flags: `--products`, `--start`, `--end`, `--skip-years`, `--key-var`, `--shard I/N`, `--dry-run`, `--max-transactions-per-window`.
- **Layout**: `data/raw/firms/{PRODUCT}/{PRODUCT}_{YYYY-MM}.csv` (products `MODIS_SP`, `VIIRS_SNPP_SP`, `VIIRS_NOAA20_SP`, `VIIRS_NOAA21_SP` and the `_NRT` equivalents for the months after SP ends). Also `manifest.json` (rows, bytes, covered dates per month), `download.log`, and `download.pid` while running. With `--shard I/N` each process writes `manifest.shardI.json`, `download.shardI.log` and `download.shardI.pid`.
- **How a month is built**: consecutive world requests of at most 5 days (the world endpoint accepts 1 to 5 days), concatenated with a single header, written to `.part` and renamed when complete. Existing months are skipped on rerun. Checked on Feb 2016 (MODIS_SP): 284146 rows, header once, 29 distinct dates, no duplicate rows, last window of 4 days ends on the 29th.
- **Endpoint**: `/api/area/csv/{KEY}/{PRODUCT}/world/{DAYS}/{YYYY-MM-DD}`. Available ranges come from `/api/data_availability/csv/{KEY}/ALL` (built-in fallback if the call fails). On 2026-11-14: MODIS_SP 2000-11-01 to 2026-08-31, VIIRS_SNPP_SP 2012-01-20 to 2026-07-31, VIIRS_NOAA20_SP 2018-04-01 to 2026-07-31, VIIRS_NOAA21_SP 2023-03-01 to 2026-07-31. NRT products cover the later months (SNPP NRT stops 2026-11-02).
- **Measured API cost**: about 36 transactions per world-day. Key limit 5000 per 10 minutes, shared with every tool using the key. The script reads `current_transactions` from `/mapserver/mapkey_status/` before each request and waits while the next request would pass 4500. A non-CSV body (for example "Invalid" or "exceeded") counts as an error and is retried with exponential backoff, six retries, then the month is skipped and listed at the end of the run.
- **Download order**: 2012-2020 MODIS and SNPP month by month, then NOAA-20 2018-04 to 2020-12, then 2021 to SP end for MODIS, SNPP and NOAA-20, then NOAA-21 SP, then NRT tails, then MODIS 2000-11 to 2011-12.
- **API plan size on 2026-11-14 (not used for the bulk)**: 641 product-months, 19416 product-days, about 699000 transactions, at least 26 hours with one key. The counter is per key (measured), so N keys give about N times the throughput (6 keys: about 4 hours). Because the archive covers every year, the API job was stopped after 48 months (2012 to 2013); those files are redundant and are checked against the archive.
- **Size (measured)**: the first 22 archive requests (2018, 2019, 2023 to 2026) are 5.6 GB of zips and about 25 GB of CSV. Expected total for all 55: about 50 GB of CSV plus about 12 GB of zips (zips can be deleted after unzipping and checking). Measured on 2026-11-14 after unzipping: 59 CSV files (archive plus `fire_nrt_*` tails), 53.3 GB, covering MODIS 2000-11-01 to 2026-11-14, S-NPP 2012-01-20 to 2026-11-02, NOAA-20 2018-04-01 to 2026-11-14, NOAA-21 2023-03-01 to 2026-11-14, with no gaps between files.
- **Unverified**: whether NRT and SP rows overlap near the boundary (the plan starts NRT the day after SP ends, so none is expected).

- **Visual design (2026-11-15)**: direction in `web/DESIGN.md`. Cool chalk land and steel water so the warm ember ramp is the only warm colour, a warm cell outline that meets 3:1 against land, hexagon legend swatches, a floating card style for map controls, and a time strip with a month cursor and flag. Light theme only. Tokens in `web/src/styles/tokens.css` and `tokens.ts`. ## 3g. Data Build (built and run, 2026-11-15)

Scripts in `pipeline/` (details, commands and timings in `pipeline/README.md`). DuckDB 2.0.0 from Python 3.14 in a venv (`pipeline/requirements.txt`), with the DuckDB `h3` community extension (works on DuckDB 2.0; h3-py is only used for checks). One command rebuilds everything: `python pipeline/build.py --data-root <folder with raw/>`. Settings and every method decision are in `pipeline/config.toml`.

| Step | Output | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| normalize | `data/processed/normalized/` Parquet per product and source file (schema in `pipeline/README.md`) | 59 CSV files, 660 million rows, 141 s, 7.0 GB |
| grid | `data/processed/atoms/`: res 6 x month x satellite with day masks, coverage tables, NRT static mask | 125 s, 1.1 GB |
| harmonize | `data/processed/harmonized/cells.parquet` (58.0 million cell-months) and `meta.json` | 223 s, 268 MB |
| publish | `web/public/firms/` (git-ignored), contract v1, `"source": "firms"` | 361 s, 2.6 GB, 47,305 files |

- **Inputs (measured)**: archive only; `data/raw/firms/` holds no API months (the build can use API months for any (product, month) the archive lacks, `use_api_months`, but the archive covered every month). SP and NRT never overlap in time (checked). Data through 2026-11-14. Record 2000-11 to 2026-11, 2026-11 partial (14 of 30 days), 2026-08 to 2026-11 provisional (NRT), 2000-11 to 2012-01 estimated.
- **Satellites (measured from the files)**: Aqua from 2002-07-04, nominal (data every day) from 2002-10; S-NPP nominal from 2012-02, last data 2026-11-02; NOAA-20 from 2018-04; NOAA-21 from 2023-03 (per-satellite values only).
- **Filters**: type 0, confidence nominal or high (MODIS: nominal 30 to 79, high 80 and above). NRT files have no `type`: a static mask (21,963 res 6 cells where at least half of the 2021 to 2025 SP detections are not type 0) replaces the type filter in NRT months.
- **Values**: active cell-days at res 6, UTC days. `modis` = days seen by Terra or Aqua. `viirs` = mean of S-NPP and NOAA-20 over those with data on every day of the month, else the one available. Harmonized values are stored as integer tenths, so overview, detail and summary sums are exact.
- **Harmonization**: S-NPP / MODIS ratio per H3 res 1 region and season, fitted on 2012-02 to 2019-12 (93 months; 2012-03 and 2016-02 dropped because a satellite had a missing day). 1,198 region-seasons use their own ratio, 400 the res 0 fallback, 466 the global one. Global ratio: DJF 2.90, MAM 3.06, JJA 2.47, SON 2.84. The 2020 to 2025 fit (not applied) is within 4 percent: DJF 3.00, MAM 3.12, JJA 2.50, SON 2.87. Terra-only ratio before 2002-10.
- **How to read the numbers**: per-cell harmonized values before 2012-02 come from a regional ratio. They are estimated, can exceed the days in the month and are zero wherever MODIS saw nothing (plan S1). Sums over areas are the reliable figure. The band is the regional calibration spread across fit years, not a per-cell error (plan S2); the error by size of area is the leave-one-year-out table.
- **Leave-one-year-out (fit years, predicted MODIS x ratio against S-NPP)**: bias 1.004 at every scale (it is a ratio of sums, so it cannot differ between scales). Mean absolute error: res 6 2.1, res 5 3.6, res 4 7.4, region 177 cell-days. Unit-months predicted zero but observed above zero: res 6 53%, res 5 47%, res 4 41%, region 15%. 75th percentile of non-zero units, predicted against observed: res 6 6.3 / 3.0, res 5 13.6 / 7.0, res 4 27.3 / 14.0. `ring7` (centre child plus ring 1 of a res 5 cell) is the res 5 cell's own 7 children, so it equals `res5`.
- **Detail coverage (D8: the whole world)**: 13,183 res 3 tiles, one for every active res 3 cell (the world box takes every H3 centre, antimeridian and poles included); detail 1.5 GB, largest tile 1.4 MB. The first build covered only Europe and the Mediterranean, the Amazon arc and the Sahel (2,024 tiles, 392 MB). Res 5 overview for all 107 active res 0 parts. Largest month files: r3 307 KB, r4 1.2 MB (over the plan's 1 MB guide; static hosts compress JSON), r5 524 KB.
- **Known issues**: 67 outage satellite-months in `meta.json`, merged into 54 ranges in `manifest.known_issues` (both counted on the build of 2026-11-15). Rule (`pipeline/config.toml`, `outage_low_day_share = 0.4`, `outage_window_days = 91`): a satellite-month with a missing day, or a day under 40% of the satellite's median daily rows over a centred 91-day window. Includes S-NPP 2022-07 and 2022-08 (5 and 10 days missing) and all of the Aqua outage of 16 August to 2 September 2020 (15 low days in 2020-08, 2 in 2020-09; the earlier rule, 25% of the month's own median, found 2). 37 outage satellite-months fall in the 2012 to 2025 baseline years; only the 19 S-NPP and NOAA-20 ones change `h` there: S-NPP 2012-03, 2012-06, 2022-06, 2022-07, 2022-08, 2023-07, 2023-11, 2024-01, 2024-05, 2024-06, 2024-07, 2024-09, 2024-11, 2025-07, 2025-11 and NOAA-20 2023-07, 2023-09, 2023-10, 2024-02. The other 18 are Terra, Aqua or NOAA-21. They are not excluded from baselines (an app change for the team). Flags are display only.
- **Measurements behind the method decisions** (details in `pipeline/README.md`):
  - D2: NOAA-20 / S-NPP cell-days 1.022, so `h` steps by about x1.011 at 2018-04 and again at 2026-11, when NOAA-20 is alone (inside the observed period, not marked estimated). The NOAA-20 to S-NPP scale is fitted like the MODIS ratio (S-NPP / NOAA-20: DJF 0.980, MAM 0.979, JJA 0.973, SON 0.981, bands within about 1%) and written to `method.json`; the team decided not to apply it (`harmonize.scale_noaa20_to_snpp = false`).
  - D2, build setting: `nominal_day_share = 1.0` makes 15 months from 2018-04 use one VIIRS satellite (2022-07, 2022-08, 2023-07, 2023-09, 2024-02, 2024-05, 2024-06, 2024-07, 2024-09, 2024-11, 2026-03, 2026-04, 2026-06, 2026-07, 2026-11), so the series switches between the mean and one satellite in those months.
  - D4: in the fit period, 7-cell places (res 5 children) have no activity in 88.1% of place-months on MODIS against 78.7% on S-NPP (9.3 points); MODIS zero with S-NPP active in 10.6%. By res 1 region: gap over 10 points in 146 regions (44% of places), 5 to 10 in 100, under 5 in 217 (median 5.6). Relative band half-width (hi - lo) / 2h of the res 1 ratios: median 0.089, 90th percentile 0.31, maximum 4.7 (global ratios 0.009 to 0.018).
  - The app's 49-cell rule for estimated months (D27, section 4, item 4) rests on the leave-one-year-out table by scale above; the zero-share gap itself is measured at 7 cells only.
- **Daily fields (contract v1, optional, proposed)**: only for the demo place, Piedmont (D16), and its neighbouring regions: res 6 cells whose centre is in `[6.5, 43.95, 9.35, 46.55]` (Piedmont: 1,017 active cells) or `[5.4, 43.4, 12.8, 46.7]` (Piedmont, Aosta Valley, Liguria, Lombardy, Emilia-Romagna and the bordering French and Swiss areas: 3,423), and the res 3, 4 and 5 cells that contain them, for the whole cell. Detail tiles carry day masks (MODIS, S-NPP, NOAA-20) for those rows, overview files `[day, value]` pairs for those cells, the summary one daily series per area (no world series), the manifest a `daily` block (areas, the res 6 count, `"days": "utc"`) and `method.json` the rule and the VIIRS weights per month. UTC days (D10). Publish checks that the days of every cell-month and every area sum exactly to the month (largest-remainder rounding in tenths). Size added: 1.8 MB (0.07%). Contract: `Vision Docs/MVP Implementation Plan.md` section 4.6.
- **Contract check**: `npm run validate-data` passes (rules 1 to 3 included) on the world build without daily fields (47,305 files, 3,828 s) and on the build with the Piedmont daily fields (47,305 files, 3,160 s). `web/public/firms/` holds the daily build.

## 3h. Forecast in the Web App (built and verified on fixtures, 2026-11-15)

- **Source**: the team's prediction model, built in a separate project; its scripts are now in `forecast/` (`01_build_firms.py` to `05_figures.py`, packages in `forecast/requirements.txt`; the folder was `src/` until 2026-11-15), documented in `Prediction.md` and `Vision Docs/PredictionEngineeringReport.md`. Its inputs (`data/datasets/MODIS-2018-2026.csv`, `VIIRS-2018-2026.csv`) were built in that project and are not produced by `pipeline/`; it was not rerun in this repository. LightGBM quantile regression (10%, 50%, 90%) on fire history, VIIRS NOAA-20 active cell-days per H3 res 4 area and month, features cut at 31 October of the year before. Its output is `forecast_2027_res4.csv` (809,064 rows: 67,422 areas x 12 months of 2027) and `metrics.json`.
- **Held-out test, as written in the model's `metrics.json`**: mean absolute error per area and month 3.39 against 3.39 for the median of past years in 2025, and 3.60 against 3.74 in 2026 January to October. 90% and 89% of area-months fall inside the 10% to 90% range. Almost all of the skill is the usual season of each area.
- **Import**: `npm run import-forecast` (`web/scripts/import-forecast.ts`) reads the CSV and writes `forecast/` into a data folder: `manifest.json`, 24 map files (res 3 and 4, median per cell) and one tile per res 1 parent with the three quantiles per res 4 area. Measured on the 2027 file: 65,332 areas with a non-zero forecast, 485 files, 17 MB. World totals per month are the sum of `clim_mean`, as in the model's report (558, 522, 534, 442, 364, 417, 582, 718, 628, 483, 400, 449 thousand cell-days, January to December). A build without `forecast/` shows no forecast.
- **Fixtures**: `npm run fixtures` writes a stand-in forecast for 2027 (quantiles of the simulated 2012 to 2025 values per res 4 area). It is not a forecast.
- **In the app**: forecast months follow "now" on the time strip as greyed, see-through bars in a dashed frame; a button next to the place name makes the forecast year the whole strip (one wide bar per month); the calendar gets a forecast row; a selected forecast month replaces the month sentence and puts the forecast on the map at res 4 (res 3 when zoomed out). Simple mode gives a sentence, the likely range and the usual value. Scientific mode adds the 10% to 90% range, a table by month, the model notes, the held-out test rows and a forecast CSV. The selected forecast month is the URL parameter `outlook`; `month` stays the record month.
- **Place rule (app side, not a team decision)**: a place that covers a res 4 area completely takes its forecast. A place that covers part of an area takes the share of the area's harmonized activity it had in the same calendar month of the baseline years (fallback: the share over all baseline months, then the share of cells). Parts of an area add up to the area forecast. The range of a place is the sum of the scaled area ranges, which is wider than the true range for several areas; sums of medians run low.
- **Units**: the forecast is NOAA-20 cell-days. The record is the mean of S-NPP and NOAA-20 where both exist (NOAA-20 / S-NPP is 1.022, section 3g), so the forecast sits about 1% above the record's scale. Not corrected.
- **Verified on fixtures**: 132 unit tests; Playwright 87 passed, 6 skipped (swipe tests skip in WebKit), in Chromium and WebKit, including the phone spec.
- **Not checked here**: the real forecast was imported into `web/public/firms/forecast` on a machine without the real data build, so the app has not been run on the real record together with the real forecast. December 2026 has neither data nor forecast and stays empty on the strip.

App-side rules beyond the plan: confidence levels (D27, decided by the user on 2026-11-15, section 3f). Low-confidence months (estimated with fewer than 49 res 6 cells, or before 2002-10) get no class (`estimated_only`) and are drawn faded (`web/src/stats/config.ts` `estimatedCompareMinCells`, `terraOnlyBefore`). The 49-cell line is set from the leave-one-year-out table (missed share 53% at res 6, 47% at res 5, 41% at res 4, 15% for regions); it is a setting, not a measured break.
